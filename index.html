KrishiScan — Handheld Farm Intelligence Device

An offline-first agritech system integrating a 12 MP multi-spectral leaf camera and a 5-sensor electrochemical soil probe. Driven by an on-device, INT8-quantized MobileNetV3 CNN model achieving 94% diagnostic accuracy for dual crop-and-soil prescriptions in under 5 seconds.

Maxon — Intelligent AI Voice Assistant

Led a engineering team to build a low-cost, bidirectional voice assistant leveraging an ESP32 microcontroller and the Xiaozhi AI cloud platform. Utilizes I2S audio protocols (INMP441/MAX98357A), Qwen-32B/35B LLM models for English/Hindi interaction, and an MCP server framework for sensor routing to Telegram.
